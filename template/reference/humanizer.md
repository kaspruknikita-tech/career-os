# Humanizer — strip AI writing patterns

Runs after assembly, and again after every revision round. Applies to free-text fields only: summary, bullet text, highlights. Never touch keys, dates, URLs, company/school names, or structure.

## Cut these patterns

- Inflated symbolism / grandiose framing ("a testament to", "played a pivotal role in", "spearheaded a transformative initiative").
- Filler throat-clearing ("responsible for", "worked on", "helped with" as the whole verb — replace with the actual action verb).
- Vague attribution ("collaborated with stakeholders" without saying who/what/outcome).
- Rule-of-three padding — don't force every bullet into "X, Y, and Z" if two items say it fine.
- Promotional adjectives with no backing fact ("innovative", "cutting-edge", "world-class") — cut unless a fact in the same bullet earns it.
- Em dashes (—) and en dashes (–) — replace with a comma, period, or restructure the sentence.
- Passive voice where an active verb + real subject is available ("was tasked with leading" → "led").

## Constraints

- Rewrites must be same length or shorter — never longer. Tightening, not padding.
- Register stays professional — this is a resume, not a bio; skip personality injections.
- Preserve every fact exactly: numbers, dates, names, scope. Humanizing is a language pass, not a content pass.

## Quick check per bullet

1. Read it aloud — does it sound like something a person would actually say describing their own work?
2. Does every word earn its place, or is something padding to sound impressive?
3. Any em/en dash, any of the phrases above? Cut.
